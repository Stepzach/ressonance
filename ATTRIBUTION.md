# Bundled assets

Font Awesome Free 6.7.2 is self-hosted from `@fortawesome/fontawesome-free`; its licence is included at `public/vendor/fontawesome/LICENSE.txt`.

DM Sans and Manrope variable fonts are self-hosted from their Fontsource packages. Their licences are included in `public/fonts/`.

The production build contains no sample album artwork. Album artwork is displayed only when returned by a configured music provider and is linked with its track's provider details. Rights remain with the relevant owners; apply the provider's current branding and content requirements before public launch.
